Tips

🚦 Rocket startAccelerate in the half-second before GO, after the third red light. Too early stalls the engine
✨ Mini-turboHold drift in a bend: sparks go blue, orange, then purple. Let go for a boost
💨 SlipstreamStay right behind another engine to get a tow
➤ Boost padsDrive over the orange arrows
👻 GhostIn Time Trial you race against your best run
